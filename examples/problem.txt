A workshop makes tables and chairs. Each table uses 3 hours of labor and 4 units of wood. Each chair uses 2 hours of labor and 1 unit of wood. The workshop has 40 labor hours and 30 units of wood available. A table earns 50 dollars of profit and a chair earns 20 dollars. Choose non-negative integer quantities of tables and chairs to maximize total profit without exceeding either resource limit.
